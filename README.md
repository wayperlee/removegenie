# RemoveGenie

Brush over people, objects, text, watermarks, or stickers. [RemoveGenie](https://removegenie.com/) rebuilds that part of the photo.

Homepage: https://removegenie.com/

## What it does

RemoveGenie is a brush-based AI photo eraser.

- [Remove people from photo](https://removegenie.com/remove-people-from-photo)
- [AI object remover](https://removegenie.com/remove-object-from-photo)
- [Remove text from image](https://removegenie.com/remove-text-from-image)
- [Remove watermark from photo](https://removegenie.com/remove-watermark-from-photo)
- [Remove sticker from image](https://removegenie.com/remove-sticker-from-image)
- [Magic eraser](https://removegenie.com/magic-eraser)

Sign in with Google to submit. New accounts get 16 credits, enough for one removal, and those credits last 30 days. Each removal costs 16 credits.

Upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP up to 20 MB. The download is a PNG, resized so the longest edge is at most 1536 px. It works best when the erased area covers less than about a third of the frame. Covered pixels are generated, not recovered. Only remove a watermark from an image you have the right to edit.

Support: support@removegenie.com
